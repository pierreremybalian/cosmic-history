The story starts at the Planck time, 5.4×10<sup>−44</sup> seconds after the Big Bang. Any earlier, gravity and quantum physics would both matter at once, and we have no tested theory that combines them.

This chapter is a picture of ideas, not of measurements. Read its shapes and colours as a sketch of what physicists suspect, not a view of what happened.
