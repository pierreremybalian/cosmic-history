Earth has oceans by about 4.4 billion years ago, and microbes appear by about 3.8 billion years ago. Around 2.4 billion years ago, microbes that harvest sunlight fill the air with oxygen, and by about 1.8 billion years ago complex cells with a nucleus have evolved.

This chapter's clock is logarithmic in years before today, so those first three billion years pass quickly and recent history gets more room.
