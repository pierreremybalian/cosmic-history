For a few minutes the whole universe is hot and dense enough to fuse nuclei, hotter than the core of the Sun but cooling and thinning fast.

To build helium, a proton and a neutron must first pair up as deuterium, the weak link in the chain. The gauge shows the mass fractions computed for each moment. Every heavier element will have to wait for stars.
