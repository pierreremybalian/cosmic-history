To build helium, a proton and a neutron must first pair up as deuterium. Until around three minutes, the heat breaks each new deuterium nucleus apart again. This is the deuterium bottleneck. Once the universe cools enough, deuterium survives and the bottleneck breaks.

Most of the action is over within the first two minutes of this chapter. The gauge shows the mass fractions computed for each moment.
