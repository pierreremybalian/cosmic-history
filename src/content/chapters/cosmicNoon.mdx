Galaxies grow by pulling in gas along the cosmic web and by merging with each other. Star formation climbs to a peak that astronomers call cosmic noon, then slowly declines.

The curve on screen shows the rate at which the whole universe makes stars. About three quarters of all the stars ever made were born during this chapter.
