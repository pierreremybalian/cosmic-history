When inflation ends, its energy turns into particles. Space fills with a plasma far hotter than the core of any star: quarks, gluons, electrons, neutrinos and light, all moving at or near the speed of light.

This chapter spans 26 powers of ten in time. For most of it the soup simply expands and cools. The big change comes near the end, when the Higgs field switches on.
