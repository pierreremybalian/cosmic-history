For most of cosmic history, the pull of matter slowed the expansion of space. About 7.7 billion years after the Big Bang that changed, and the expansion began to speed up.

Galaxies and clusters hold together; only the space between clusters stretches. This chapter ends 4.6 billion years ago, just before our Sun is born.
