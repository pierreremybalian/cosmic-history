Our Solar System formed from gas and dust that earlier generations of stars had enriched with carbon, oxygen, silicon and iron.

These events are dated precisely from meteorites and ancient minerals. The motions you see, a spinning disk and a planet cooling from molten rock, are modelled and greatly sped up.
