About 540 million years ago, in the Cambrian explosion, animals with shells, eyes and legs appear in a geological blink. Plants and animals move onto land, and after the worst mass extinction in Earth's history, 252 million years ago, the first dinosaurs appear.

This chapter's clock is logarithmic in years before today, so recent history gets more room.
