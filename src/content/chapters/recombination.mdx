As the fog cools, electrons finally settle onto nuclei and the universe turns from opaque to transparent. The light released then has travelled ever since.

This moment is usually quoted as 380,000 years after the Big Bang; the model here puts the last scattering at about 370,000 years. At the end you see real data: Planck's map of that light across the whole sky.
