Inflation is the idea that, very early on, space went through a burst of runaway expansion. It explains why the universe looks so smooth and so flat on large scales, and where its first unevenness came from.

The scene shows growth of about 10<sup>26</sup> times, close to the minimum inflation needs. The real amount could be far larger, and the timing is uncertain. The ripples follow the pattern measured in the oldest light.
