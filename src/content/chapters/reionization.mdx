The neutral hydrogen left over from the dark ages absorbs ultraviolet light. As galaxies and black holes switch on, their light strips the electrons from that hydrogen again, one growing bubble at a time.

When the bubbles join up, the space between galaxies is clear. The rest of cosmic history plays out in this transparent universe.
